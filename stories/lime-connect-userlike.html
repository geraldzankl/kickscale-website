Lime Connect

How the Lime Connect sales team reclaimed 195 working days per year with AI

Saving 2 hours per employee each week, our 15 sales consultants spend less time on administration and more time closing deals.

IN THIS STORY

Challenges

  • Manual, time-consuming note-taking during client meetings led to duplicate work and reduced engagement
  • Handwritten notes had to be manually transferred to the CRM after every meeting, taking about 10 minutes per session
  • Key points were sometimes missed while switching between taking notes and active listening
  • Difficult to remain fully present on camera while typing notes—especially in meeting booths without dual monitors
  • Challenging to quickly retrieve specific information from past meetings
  • Lack of standardization in the sales team's meeting approaches and questioning

Usage

Lime Connect has integrated Kickscale into every client meeting across the entire sales team. Working closely with her manager, Gina configured various meeting types for different stages of the sales process:

  • Initial calls
  • Follow-up meetings
  • Quotation meetings
  • Product demos
  • Check-in calls (for account management)

The team uses Kickscale’s AI search function to quickly find specific information from past conversations, such as pricing discussions or customer pain points. Recordings are also shared with clients when colleagues who couldn't attend want to watch the meeting later. The team recently made meetings "open" internally to facilitate mutual learning.

Impact

  1. 2 hours saved per week per sales rep (30 hours weekly for the 15-person team = 195 working days annually)
  2. Increased productivity: Ability to handle more deals in pipeline coding sessions thanks to faster information access
  3. 100% focus on the customer during meetings - no distractions from note-taking
  4. Instant self-improvement: Direct AI coaching feedback on performance metrics after every meeting
  5. Improved manager collaboration: Better transparency and more efficient coaching sessions with data-driven insights
  6. Standardized sales process: Clear process structure (initial call → demo → follow-up → quotation) leads to higher closing probability and more reliable forecasting
  7. Team learning culture: Colleagues can review each other's meetings to learn best practices
  8. Better meeting preparation: AI search allows for a quick review of conversation context before follow-ups

Why Kickscale

  • Easy implementation: Simple setup - just add meeting time and video conferencing link
  • User-friendly interface: Clean, intuitive design without overwhelming options
  • Customization options: Ability to brand the notetaker with company colors and logo for a seamless look
  • Trial phase: The ability to test before committing helped with team adoption
  • Clear structure: Well-organized tabs for notes, scorecards, coaching insights, and questions

Features used

Kickscale helps sales teams at Lime Connect become more efficient and effective, leading to higher conversion rates.

  • AI call recording & summary: Automatic, reliable notes with key points captured
  • AI search: Quick retrieval of specific information from past meeting conversations
  • AI sales coaching: Real-time feedback on meeting performance and question quality
  • Meeting type configurations: Customized templates for different sales process stages
  • CRM integration: Streamlined documentation workflow
  • Team sharing & learning: Internal visibility for peer learning and best-practice sharing
