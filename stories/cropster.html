Cropster provides software solutions for the entire coffee value chain—from farmers in Africa and Latin America to roasteries and cafes worldwide. With customers on three continents and a sales team operating across US, European, and APAC time zones, VP of Sales Martin Dalberg-Andersen faced a difficult challenge: How do you coach and develop a global team when you can't be on every call? "I was managing the European team, which was manageable," Martin explains. "But when my US colleague left in May and I took over globally, I realized I would have to work 24 hours a day to join calls and understand how my team was selling across all these time zones."

The Challenge

  • Impossible scalability: 12 employees across North and South America, Europe, and APAC meant 24/7 coverage
  • Administrative burden: Sales reps spent too much time on notes & follow-ups after calls instead of selling
  • Lack of standardization: No consistent method to identify coaching needs within the global team
  • Product feedback gap: The product team complained about insufficient feedback from customer conversations
  • Discovery deficit: 70% of inside sales reps jumped straight to the demo without properly qualifying

Why Cropster chose Kickscale

When evaluating, Martin prioritized three critical factors:

  • the ability to scale coaching across time zones,
  • seamless automation to reduce administrative work, and
  • GDPR compliance for European customers.

Martin shares: "Having direct access to the Kickscale team and knowing that I can provide feedback directly to the product team—you never get that with a larger platform. This accessibility made all the difference."

European data hosting and GDPR compliance gave Cropster’s CTO and CFO confidence in the security approach, while Martin’s existing relationship with the Kickscale team ensured he received the necessary customization and support for a complex, global deployment.

"I can't be on every single sales call. But I find it important that I can coach them and provide feedback on how they can improve. Kickscale gives us this immediate feedback loop while simultaneously helping the sales team reduce their admin tasks." - Martin Dalberg-Andersen, VP of Sales, Cropster

The Solution: AI-powered global sales enablement

Reports/Insights

🎓 Cross-departmental insights

The product team accesses call recordings to understand feature feedback. Marketing analyzes keyword trends and customer language for campaign development. New team members use recorded calls for onboarding and training.

Implementation & Adoption

Martin took a strategic approach to the Kickscale rollout, starting with a trial involving 2-3 team members in July/August. The feedback was immediately positive. Sales reps appreciated the instant insights and time savings.

"No one pushed back," said Martin. "A few questioned the scoring at first, which is why we added different call types. But once it was clear that this wasn't about performance reviews, but about making them better salespeople, everyone was on board."

The key to successful adoption was transparency: Martin explicitly communicated that low call scores would not trigger performance reviews. Instead, they would identify training opportunities and provide targeted coaching to help sales reps improve.

Today, Kickscale has spread beyond the sales team. Product managers use it to aggregate customer feedback on new features. Marketing analyzes buzzword frequency and customer language patterns. Martin is even rolling out seats for the customer support team to improve how they handle support tickets and calls.

The transformation: from product pushers to consultants who deliver value

The most significant change Martin observed wasn't just in metrics; it was in sales behavior. Before Kickscale, inside sales reps typically handled bottom-of-funnel leads who were ready to buy. This led to a dangerous pattern: they would jump straight into product demos without fully understanding the customer's needs.

"The sales team was minimizing their chances of closing larger deals", Martin explains. "If they only sold a product on day one, they were leaving money on the table. Now, we close deals faster, close larger deals, and can upsell more quickly because the reps understand the importance of discovery."

A rep based in Florida took ownership of his improvement by checking specific metrics after every call. "He looks at how long he spoke, whether he spoke too fast, and then consciously works on slowing down and articulating the value better in his next calls", Martin shares. "This type of self-coaching is exactly what we wanted to enable."

The shift from product-focused to value-focused selling was transformative. Reps now spend more time on value-based sales approaches and understand not just what customers need today, but which additional products could solve future problems.

Beyond sales: Organization-wide value

What started as a sales coaching tool has become a company-wide asset at Cropster:

Product development

The product team uses Kickscale to aggregate customer feedback on new features and understand how sales reps explain product value in real conversations. This feedback loop has eliminated the historical complaint about insufficient input from customer-facing teams.

Marketing intelligence

Martin creates reports on the buzzwords and keywords most frequently used across all customer conversations. This insight directly informs the marketing team about which language resonates with customers and which topics generate the most interest.

Onboarding & Training

New hires in sales and marketing teams use recorded calls to understand customer conversations, product positioning, and effective sales techniques. This significantly accelerates onboarding time.

Excellent Customer Support

Cropster is now rolling out Kickscale to its customer support team to help manage support tickets and calls using the same coaching methodology that worked for sales.
