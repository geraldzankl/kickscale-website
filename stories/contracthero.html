How exactly does AI work at ContractHero?

AI is used to gain insights into sales conversations

  • ContractHero has connected their calendars with Kickscale AI.
  • With every conversation, they extract the most important information, such as:
    • Automatic summary & next steps
    • Why did the customer buy?
    • Which features are missing?
    • What objections were raised? and more.

Building a customer-centric organization

  • The team at ContractHero doesn't make assumptions. Instead, they use the information they gain from sales conversations to make decisions.
  • They distribute this information to teams across the organization:
    • Sales: Here is what we can work on
    • Management: Why did the customer buy? What are their real needs?
    • Product / Development: Here are feature requests or missing functionality
    • Marketing: How did they find us and why do they like our solutions?
