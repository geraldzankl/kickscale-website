How Finmatics turned 7 years of wasted data into sales excellence

By saving 10% of time per salesperson, creating permanently searchable customer conversations, and generating cross-departmental intelligence for product, sales, and leadership teams

IN THIS STORY

Challenges

  • Seven years of market intelligence rendered practically worthless - a 20-person call center contacted over 40,000 tax advisors, but CRM data was unreliable due to inconsistent entries, misunderstandings, and mandatory fields filled with guesswork
  • No way to verify what was actually discussed in customer meetings or to access historical conversation data
  • Salespeople spent 10%+ of their time on manual admin work (notes, follow-up emails, CRM updates) instead of customer conversations
  • Sales leaders could only shadow one call per week per salesperson - insufficient for qualitative coaching for over 30 sales staff
  • Salespeople talked too much (60-65% of meeting time) instead of listening and asking discovery questions
  • Subjective performance assessment without objective data on what actually happens in sales calls
  • 100 employees distributed globally across multiple countries with no systematic way to share customer insights across teams

Usage

Finmatics implemented Kickscale for ~30 users in direct sales, partner sales, and customer success using a strategic three-phase approach.

In Phase 1 (months 1-12), they focused purely on productivity - using the AI notetaker for all video meetings and integrating Aircall for phone call recordings to build a foundation of raw data while the team adjusted to the new process.

In Phase 2 (current), they trained the Kickscale AI on their individual SPEARS sales framework (Situation, Problem, Impact, Relevance, Steps) to analyze every call and provide salespeople with immediate feedback on their performance after the conversation.

Phase 3 (planned) will activate AI-powered forecasting, combining HubSpot deal data with conversation intelligence to provide neutral deal assessments.

Beyond sales, their product team analyzes over 200 customer conversations monthly to extract feature requests and complaints without having to attend meetings or interview salespeople.

Impact

  1. 10%+ time savings per salesperson - Immediate elimination of manual note-taking, email drafting, and CRM data entry
  2. Salesperson talk time reduced from 60-65% to 45% - More effective discovery through better questioning and listening
  3. Seven years of lost market data recovered - Every conversation captured as "raw data gold" that can be analyzed from any angle at any time
  4. Scalable 30:1 coaching ratio - Every call analyzed with immediate feedback versus manual shadowing of 1 call per week per salesperson
  5. Objective performance insights - Clear correlation identified between sales stage progression and win rates; salespeople who skip stages have measurably lower conversion
  6. Cross-departmental intelligence - Product team extracts insights from 400+ monthly conversations; CFO receives better forecasting data; RevOps optimizes processes
  7. Culture of self-improvement - Salespeople receive immediate AI coaching after every call for daily development
  8. Investor-ready operations - Professional data infrastructure and processes proven for investor due diligence

Why Kickscale

  • Native HubSpot integration: Seamlessly extends their HubSpot platform with automatic field mapping and data synchronization
  • Works with existing phone system: Integrates with Aircall to analyze video meetings and phone calls in one platform
  • Raw data foundation: All conversations permanently stored and searchable - new questions can be asked of old conversations at any time
  • Custom framework training: AI learns their specific SPEARS sales methodology for relevant, actionable feedback
  • Scales with company size: From simple notetaking for early-stage teams to revenue intelligence for scale-ups before their next funding round
  • Phased implementation approach: Start with simple productivity gains (months 1-12) before activating advanced intelligence features - crucial for change management

Features used

Kickscale helps the 30-person go-to-market team at Finmatics eliminate admin work, improve sales quality, and extract cross-departmental insights from every customer conversation.

  • AI call recording & transcription: Automatic capture of all video meetings and phone calls (via Aircall integration)
  • AI meeting summaries: Eliminates manual note-taking and meeting documentation
  • Custom sales framework scoring: SPEARS methodology trained into AI for consistent evaluation of every call
  • AI sales coaching: Immediate post-call feedback on talk time, stage progression, question quality, and next-meeting booking
  • HubSpot field mapping: Automatic extraction and synchronization of key data points (number of employees, pain points, objections) into custom CRM fields
  • AI search across all conversations: Product team searches hundreds of monthly conversations for topics, feature requests, and complaints
  • Team sharing: Recordings accessible to sales, product, leadership, and other functions for insights

Aggregated analytics: Leadership dashboard shows performance patterns and coaching opportunities across the entire team
