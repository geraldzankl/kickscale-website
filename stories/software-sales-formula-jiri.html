From admin chaos to maximum customer focus: Jiri Siklar's AI strategy

With automated note tracking and objective AI coaching, sales reps save massive amounts of time on CRM maintenance and continuously improve their win rates.

IN THIS STORY

Challenges

  • High administrative burden: Studies show that sales reps spend 70–80% of their time on administrative tasks rather than active selling.
  • Lack of external perspective: Sellers are often "stuck in their own heads" during conversations and find it difficult to objectively evaluate their own performance (e.g., talk-to-listen ratio, questioning).
  • Time-consuming sales coaching: Manually analyzing call recordings is extremely labor-intensive for trainers, making it difficult to identify individual skill gaps and bottlenecks.
  • Difficult to measure: It is hard for sellers to verify whether they have successfully implemented feedback in their next call.

Usage

Jiri Siklar (16 years of IT & software sales experience at companies like AWS and MongoDB) used Kickscale extensively himself while working in enterprise sales for a US corporation. Today, he recommends its systematic use as the foundation for excellent sales coaching.

In practice, this means:

Sales reps record every meeting and check the Kickscale coaching tool weekly. They analyze their feedback, select two specific constructive points (e.g., incomplete qualification in the MEDDIC framework or a talk-to-listen ratio that is too high), and set these as their focus for the following week.

At the end of the week, they use the AI analysis to objectively check whether these metrics have improved.

Impact

  • Maximum focus on revenue: No more manual meeting notes for customers or manual CRM updates; the time saved goes directly into "face time" with new customer meetings.
  • Higher productivity & conversion: Through continuous AI-supported self-coaching, sellers improve their skills faster, maximizing revenue generated relative to salary.
  • Scalable training: The AI filters out the biggest challenges and skill gaps from meetings in advance. The trainer only needs to validate and fine-tune, allowing them to effectively manage a much larger volume of meetings.
  • Objective measurement: Sellers immediately see, in a transparent and unbiased way, whether they have asked the right sales questions and properly covered frameworks.

Why Kickscale

  • The perfect addition for trainers: Kickscale does not replace coaching; it makes it extremely targeted and effective through a structured data foundation.
  • Continuous support: The tool provides almost cost-free, ongoing coaching for every single customer interaction.
  • Background automation: Seamless synchronization with the CRM, without sales reps having to interrupt their workflow.

Features used

  • AI Call Recording & Summary: Automatic meeting notes and direct synchronization with the CRM system.
  • AI Sales Coaching: Evaluation of frameworks (like MEDDIC), talk-to-listen ratios, patience, and sales questions for immediate self-improvement.
  • Skill Gap Analysis: AI-powered filtering of the biggest bottlenecks in calls for more efficient management and trainer feedback.
"Sales reps cost a lot of money and should have as much face time with customers as possible. Any administrative task I can offload to an AI is time gained. Because we are always stuck in our own heads, this external perspective provided by a tool like Kickscale is incredibly important. I immediately have an objective metric thanks to the AI." – Jiri Siklar, Sales Trainer & Host of the Sales Formula Podcast
