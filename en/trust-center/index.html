10 FAQ
01Where is Kickscale hosted?+

The available Trust Center information lists Google Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany as the hosting provider and location. Processing locations of other providers must be considered separately. The agreed data processing agreement and related documents determine the arrangement for your specific setup.

02Which AI models are used, and are conversation data used for training?+

The Kickscale AI Model Statement and the agreed contractual documents are the basis for this review. Processing conversations and training a provider's model are separate questions. The available material does not support a blanket statement for every model and setup. Our privacy team can provide an up-to-date confirmation for your configuration.

03How does recording consent work?+

You can enable recording-consent emails in the workspace settings. External participants can accept or decline. Review their responses before the meeting and follow up directly when a response is missing. If someone declines, you must ensure that the notetaker does not join. Agree the process with your privacy team.

Help Center guide ↗ (Opens in a new tab)

04What should we do if someone declines or a meeting is confidential?+

Do not schedule a recording for these meetings, and ensure that the notetaker does not join. Review bot scheduling and your meeting settings. A calendar event marked private is not a confirmed automatic exclusion in the material available here. Technical recording capability does not replace any required approval.

Help Center guide ↗ (Opens in a new tab)

05What does the calendar integration access, and can we use Kickscale without it?+

The calendar integration helps assign meetings and participants to the notetaker. Review the requested permissions during setup. Calendar access and email integrations must be assessed separately. Disabling a recording does not necessarily stop calendar metadata from being synchronised. Alternatively, the bot can be scheduled manually.

Help Center guide ↗ (Opens in a new tab)

06Who can see conversations and analyses?+

Visibility depends on the configured roles, teams and sharing settings. Before rollout, define who should see which conversations and analyses, and verify the configuration. Requirements such as anonymised team reporting or specific restrictions for managers must be confirmed for your setup rather than assumed to be enabled.

07How long are recordings, transcripts and analyses retained?+

The technical discussions reviewed describe configurable deletion and retention settings. Agree the required periods for each data type with your responsible teams and have the current workspace implementation confirmed. Contractual retention periods and end-of-contract rules remain authoritative; a pilot-specific arrangement does not automatically apply to all customers.

Data processing agreement including approved subprocessors ↗PDF link: verification pending

08How do we handle a specific deletion request?+

Identify the affected conversations or data and review the available deletion options with your administrator. Our privacy team can help clarify the scope and contractual periods. Include copies already transferred to your CRM or other systems; deletion of those copies must be checked separately.

09Which subprocessors and data transfers should we review?+

The DPA is listed as including approved subprocessors. The library also has entries for AssemblyAI, Recall, Microsoft Ireland, Langfuse and Google. Review the current documents to establish which providers and countries apply to your setup and which transfer basis is agreed. A listing alone does not confirm Data Privacy Framework certification.
