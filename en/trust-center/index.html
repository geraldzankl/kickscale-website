03 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Where is Kickscale hosted?

The hosting provider listed in the Privacy Center is Google Cloud. The specified location is Frankfurt, Germany.

Where can I find the ISO 27001 certificate?

The ISO 27001 certificate is listed in the Kickscale Privacy Center. Open the “ISO 27001 certification” entry in the document library to access the Privacy Center.

Which data processing agreements are available?

The list includes the Kickscale DPA with approved subprocessors and DPA documents for AssemblyAI, Recall, Microsoft Ireland, Langfuse, and Google. Refer to the original documents for the binding terms.

Where can I find information about AI models?

In the “Kickscale-AI-Models-Statement” document. Find it under “AI & models” in the document library and in the Privacy Center.

Does this overview replace the original documents?

No. This overview helps you navigate the information. Consult the original documents in the Privacy Center for complete details and binding terms.
