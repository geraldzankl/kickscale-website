Implementation at a glance

Our guided implementation covers discovery, technical configuration, training and production rollout. The specific deliverables and quantities are agreed in the order form or statement of work.

Month 1 · Foundation

Kickoff, success metrics, workspace configuration, privacy settings, calendars and CRM connection.

Month 2 · Adoption

Meeting types, coaching and intelligence setup, initial agent workflows, pilot users and role-based training.

Month 3 · Go-live

Workflow validation, user rollout, usage review, refinement, handover and go-live acceptance.

What we configure

Program management & kickoff

Stakeholder workshop, implementation owners, objectives, success indicators, rollout milestones and responsibility matrix.

Workspace & conversation capture

Workspace, team structure, permission roles, privacy, sharing, recording preferences, languages and meeting types.

CRM & integrations

Connection to supported CRM and meeting systems, identity matching, field mappings, synchronization rules, approval flows and sample-data tests.

AI Assistant

Meeting templates, summaries, extraction rules, follow-up formats, CRM updates and end-user workflows.

AI Coaching

Agreed scorecards, qualification methodology, feedback permissions, reporting and human review where permitted.

Deal & Revenue Intelligence

Deal association, qualification frameworks (MEDDPICC or equivalent), health indicators, KPIs, pipeline reporting and forecasting configuration.

AI Agents & Automations

Prioritization of initial use cases, triggers, scopes, output fields, approvals, test runs, credit monitoring and release into production.

Analytics & tracking

Adoption baselines, core dashboards, relevant KPIs and validation of tracking and reporting accuracy.

Enablement & training

Training is adapted to user roles and scheduled jointly with the customer.

  • Administrator onboarding: access, security, integrations, configuration, quality checks and maintenance.
  • Sales and customer-facing user onboarding: recording, meeting preparation, assistant, CRM sync and follow-up workflows.
  • Managers and team leads: intelligence, forecasting, scorecards, dashboards and review routines.
  • Agents workshops: workflow ownership, dry runs, safe execution, approvals, credit monitoring and troubleshooting.
  • Learning resources: documentation, recordings or guides, agreed follow-up Q&A and transition to ongoing support.

Go-live readiness & acceptance

The shared go-live checklist confirms the agreed scope is ready for production:

Named administrators and deployment owners trained
Agreed user groups invited and enabled
Supported CRM connection tested end-to-end
Meeting capture and privacy settings validated
Defined scorecards and intelligence views reviewed
Initial agreed automations tested with approval controls
Open blocking issues tracked and assigned
Handover notes, escalation path and support channel shared

Responsibilities & scope

Kickscale leads product configuration and knowledge transfer. The customer provides timely administrator access, technical owners, necessary consents, field definitions, CRM permissions, test users and feedback. Third-party and security approvals remain with the relevant owners.

Custom integrations, extensive data migrations, bespoke software engineering, additional workshops or new automations beyond the agreed implementation scope are quoted separately. AI credits consumed by workflows are billed according to the contract. The 90-day timeline depends on timely customer inputs and third-party availability.

Onboarding FAQs

Can we go live before day 90?

Yes. The 90-day period is a maximum implementation window for the agreed rollout. Teams can go live in phases once their configurations and integrations are validated.

Who needs to participate?

A customer project owner, CRM administrator, relevant IT/security stakeholders, sales or CS leaders and selected pilot users.

How many agents are included?

The number, complexity and scope of initial agents are specified in the order form or implementation statement of work. AI usage credits are separate unless explicitly included.

Does onboarding include all CRM customizations?

It includes the agreed supported CRM connection and mapping. Extensive custom development and additional instances are separately scoped.

What if employee coaching cannot be enabled?

Assistant, intelligence and automation capabilities can be rolled out independently. Coaching configurations are subject to the customer's legal and organizational approval.
