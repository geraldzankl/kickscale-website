Meeting capture, access, configuration, integrations, CRM synchronization, reporting, platform and agent troubleshooting.

Escalation and communication

Level 1 · Support desk

Ticket intake, severity confirmation, first investigation and solution or workaround guidance.

Level 2 · Customer Success & technical specialist

Coordinated diagnosis, integration/configuration expertise and stakeholder updates.

Level 3 · Product & Engineering

Complex defects, incident ownership, restoration actions and engineering investigations.

Level 4 · Management

Executive-level escalation for unresolved and materially business-critical incidents.

Incident updates: P1 at least every two business hours while under active investigation; P2 at least once per business day. Restoration and resolution estimates are communicated when reasonably available, not contractually guaranteed.

Support FAQs

Is support included with the subscription?

Basic or premium support entitlements depend on the purchased plan. The paid Business Support package adds the agreed service-level commitments, dedicated coordination and structured growth support.

Is 24/7 support included?

No. The Business Support package covers defined business hours. An enhanced out-of-hours critical-incident service must be separately contracted.

Does the SLA guarantee resolution within four hours?

No. SLA response is the time to qualified human acknowledgement and triage. Resolution timing depends on severity, engineering effort and third parties.

What information should a support request contain?

Describe the incident and business impact, affected users, timestamps, meeting links or IDs, CRM type and any screenshots/error messages. Avoid sharing credentials or unneeded personal data.

Who is my escalation contact?

Named Customer Success, technical and management contacts are confirmed during onboarding and recorded in the customer's support addendum.

Can support build unlimited custom automations?

No. Standard troubleshooting and guidance are included as agreed. Major new configurations, custom software development, extra integration work and credit consumption are separately scoped.
